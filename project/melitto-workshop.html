The workshop

Every Melitto piece starts as a conversation and ends as something you can wear, hug, or use at the kitchen table. Here is how it happens.

How a piece is made

  1. Idea and sketch. We talk about what you need and draw the design.
  2. Measurements. Melina adjusts the pattern to your size or your idea.
  3. Fabric. You pick a fabric that fits the piece and how you will use it.
  4. Cutting and sewing. Every seam is sewn by hand on Melina's machine.
  5. Finishing and delivery. Last details, a final check, and the piece is yours.
